They authored differentiated kick/snare/hat rhythms, bass gates and rests, short recurring motifs and sustained triads; preserved fixed and locked material through variation; developed a duplicate section without changing the original; recorded short input fixtures into protected phrases; launched sections at bar boundaries; and auditioned, returned from and accepted a scoped live variation.

The fourth scenario authored an 88-event, four-bar A-minor sketch with kick/backbeat/accented hats, bass rests and varied gates, Am/F/G/Am held harmony, and a six-note motif. Its scoped arpeggio preserved a locked anchor. Silent keyboard input and CC64 produced three protected notes with sustain-aware durations; live audition/return, bar launching, take replay and bank recall passed. Independent parsing matched the authored 88-note and arranged 296-note exports exactly. Metadata rename regressions now also require byte-identical exported music before and after renaming unlocked, off-grid and out-of-scale authored notes.

The orchestrator replayed the third reviewer's saved authored/generated projects through the corrected CLI and that reviewer's independent binary MIDI parser. Every accepted note tuple still matches. Empty and sparse sections, silent arrangements, mixed meters, repeats and take terminal timestamps have binary-parsed regressions.

The sixth scenario used visible controls to build a D-minor sketch at 112 BPM: syncopated bass, D-minor/B-flat pad voicings, a captured answer phrase, and a contrasting section with a muted bass and raised pulse register. Bar launches, audition/return, A×2/B/A arrangement and named bank recall were exercised. Independent parsing found 210 balanced note pairs, six tracks and exactly eight bars. The orchestrator reran the sixth reviewer's exact viewport harness in a separate artifact directory against `d31a64c`: the chord now shows 6/6 notes, and group transposition immediately shows 15/15 selected notes. The seventh reviewer independently authored a 16-bar G-Dorian/C-Dorian sketch with A×2/B/B form, contrasting density, Cm–F–Gm–Cm harmony and a captured G4/A4/B-flat4 phrase. ## Acceptance limits

The application supports intentional section-based composition and prepared performance. Monitoring and recording arm remain coupled; changing the target requires Stop. Rehearsing over a backing and punching into recording without stopping, count-in, retrospective capture and seamless cross-song transitions are not established workflows in this version.

Headless rendering and virtual MIDI do not establish native attended usability, native file-picker behavior, human downbeat capture, audible groove/timbre, physical interface timing, unplug/reconnect, sleep/resume, hour-long use or DAW import. The final reviewer used public BringIntoView to expose some controls before pointer input, so human scrolling/discovery efficiency is not fully established. Its early RunJobs-only live captures did not advance UI timers and are excluded; final live evidence used bounded Dispatcher.MainLoop intervals. Instrument sound-state recall remains outside scope. Those are explicit acceptance limits, not evidence that the software failed those tests.
